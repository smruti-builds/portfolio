About

I work where product, program and process meet. In smaller teams that has meant owning the product itself, from vision and roadmap to prioritization and cost trade-offs. In larger organisations it has meant strengthening the processes, governance and reporting around products that are already in market.

My career began in engineering, at NetApp and Qlogic. I co-founded VISpectrum, a machine vision venture, then worked in systems and QA at VMware, as a business analyst on embedded automation, as a product consultant on digital products, and as a co-founder of Art Infused Workplaces. After a career break, I joined Morgan Stanley as a Senior Manager in Investment Banking Operations. I am currently a mentee in the Women in Big Data Shakti Mentoring Program.

2026 – present
Bangalore

Workplace communication prototype In progress

Mentee, Women in Big Data Shakti Mentoring Program (Cohort 2026)

Problem: communication at work, for example in appraisal meetings. The work started from the article The Anatomy of a Muted Mind and focuses on psychological safety, feedback quality and behavioral decision-making.

My contribution so far: I proposed turning the process into a game. The ideas are:

  • A threat meter that gives immediate visual feedback on psychological safety.
  • ICA cards or poker-style exercises that teach the vocabulary through quick team exercises.
  • Achievements and streaks that reinforce the habit over time.
  • An AI feedback coach that scores feedback in real time and suggests action-focused rewrites.
