Project AURA — The Emotional OTP: Can Your Body Crack the Code? Live demo →

Lead System Designer & Bio-Signal Modeling · GitHub

Born from WiBD mentoring conversations with Ranjitha around her The Anatomy of a Muted Mind, Project Aura began with discussions on neuroscience, emotional processing, and the elusive 30-second pause. In her writing, that pause comes from something as ordinary as asking who would like tea. I told her I had not been able to take a 30-second pause in the middle of racing emotions, and she suggested an emotional one-time passcode (OTP) could help.

Emotions vary, situations evolve, and each of us responds differently. Could physiological signals offer clues to the emotions underlying these responses? Aura begins by examining body battery scores and exploring whether changes in heart rate are worth investigating as potential indicators of an emotional surge—not as proof of an emotion, but as patterns worth understanding.

What I built and explored

  • An interactive simulator of wearable sensor streams (heart rate, HRV, electrodermal activity, respiration and motion) that compares a classic four-stage detection pipeline with a three-dimensional parallel stress-detection model.
  • A different way of reading smartwatch data: the same heart rate and blood pressure readings, treated as patterns that might point to different emotional states, with time as an added dimension.
  • A first set of emotional conditions or states to test those patterns against.

Where it stands: this is at the hypothesis stage. Any pattern would need to be analysed with clinical psychologists and psychiatrists before it is treated as a signal of an emotional state.
